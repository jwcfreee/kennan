Featured Project

NANIK — AI Commerce Operations Platform

I designed and built NANIK as a production SaaS for e-commerce operations. It connects with Shopify, brings operational data into one workspace, provides analytics and AI-assisted auditing, and uses a human-controlled Action Queue so recommendations remain under operator approval.

The project covers product design, application architecture, authentication, workspace permissions, Shopify OAuth and synchronization, operational workflows, deployment, and SaaS billing infrastructure.
